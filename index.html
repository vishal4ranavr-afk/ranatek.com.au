The technology

What are electronic shelf labels?

An electronic shelf label (ESL) is a small digital price tag that replaces paper tickets and handwritten cards. It uses e-paper, the same crisp, glare-free display as an e-reader, so prices, specials and allergen details stay sharp under bright cabinet lighting.

Each label receives its updates wirelessly from a small gateway hub that is linked to your POS. Change something once and every label that shows it updates, with no printing, no cutting and no walking the counter with a roll of sticky tape.
